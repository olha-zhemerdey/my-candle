Our Scents

Lavender Driftwood

A fresh, woody fragrance with a mysterious marine accent. It offers a new take on classic lavender, blending this timeless floral note with masculine hints of cypress, cedar, and amber.

Ambered Vanille

The fragrance opens with creamy coconut and sweet sugar. In the heart — radiant vanilla, complemented by a light floral hint of jasmine. The composition is rounded off with warm amber, aromatic patchouli, and soft musk.

Rose & Musk

A free and refined floral fragrance that captures the essence of the entire rose — from petals to stem. Top notes of basil and grapefruit evoke fresh greenery, unfolding into a vibrant heart of rich roses and delicate magnolia blooms. Base notes of sweet and earthy patchouli complete this elegant scent.
